YOUR DAILY CARD

What are you being invited to notice today?

You don't need to know what you're looking for. Simply pull a card, take a moment with what you see, and notice what catches your attention.

Make it your own

There is no right way to interpret your card.

Rather than giving you a predetermined definition of what your card means, I invite you to explore it for yourself.

Look at the visual. Read the words. Notice what immediately catches your attention, creates a reaction, or feels relevant to where you are right now.

Your interpretation may be completely different from someone else's, and that's exactly what makes the experience personal. Every now and then, you'll find that the meaning that emerges is more useful than an interpretation someone else has given you.

Take it a little further

Let your card become the starting point.

You don't have to do anything with your card. But if something about it speaks to you, let it become a foundation for whatever you need today.

Intention

Use your card as a theme or focus for the day ahead.

Meditation

Sit with the image or words and notice what arises.

Tapping

Use your card as a starting point for an EFT tapping session.

Journaling

Let the card open up a question, thought, or reflection.
